Our story

A brand with Rajasthan in its heart.

RABARAN is a celebration of the colour, craftsmanship and cultural spirit of Rajasthan. Our identity draws inspiration from heritage motifs and the traditions that bring families together around a shared meal.

We believe a food brand should feel as memorable as the flavours it represents: warm, welcoming, considered and proud of its roots. We are building RABARAN one thoughtful step at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Are RABARAN products available to buy yet?

Not on this pre-launch website. Product availability, prices and purchase links will be added after the official product launch.

Where can I find ingredients and pack details?

Verified ingredients, net quantity, allergen information and applicable food-label details will be published with each product when available.

How can I enquire about retail or wholesale?

Use the customer-care form and choose “Retail / wholesale partnership.” It will prepare an email in your email app.

How do I contact customer support?

Use the enquiry form above. The official customer-care email and other support channels must be configured before publishing.

Can I place an order on this website?

Ordering and payment are not enabled yet. This website is currently a brand preview and will need a secure checkout system before accepting online orders.
